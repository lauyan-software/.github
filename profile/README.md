# Lauyan Software

French independent software publisher, based in Rambouillet since 2005. We build tools that let small businesses, associations and independents create and run their own online presence, without code.

## TOWeb

Website and online shop builder for **Mac and Windows**. You write directly on the page, pick a complete style, and publish to the host of your choice: the site stays a folder that belongs to you, and works offline. Free Standard edition, no subscription.

→ [lauyan.com](https://www.lauyan.com/en/) · [Documentation](https://www.lauyan.com/en/documentation/) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@LauyanSoftwareEN)

## PageXL

Hosted builder for one-page websites (landing pages, personal and business sites, small online stores), assembled from editable sections in the browser, with no code. You can start building before creating an account, and the editor works on a phone or tablet as well as on a desktop.

→ [pagexl.com](https://pagexl.com) · [Try the editor](https://pagexl.com/builder/) · [Changelog](https://changelog-en.pagexl.com/)

## WebLegal.ai

Generates the legal documents of a website (privacy policy, cookie policy, terms of use, terms of sale) in many languages, for a one-time price, with a free compliance scanner.

→ [weblegal.ai](https://weblegal.ai)

## Mailzy

Hosted web app for building and sending email newsletters, for small businesses and website owners who run occasional campaigns. Campaigns are built from ready-made responsive templates, sent through managed infrastructure, and billed as prepaid send credits with no subscription.

→ [mailzy.cc](https://mailzy.cc/)

## Screenzy

Free online tool that turns screenshots and images into polished visuals, with frames, filters and backgrounds, and exports them without watermarks.

→ [screenzy.io](https://screenzy.io)

## Open source

**[weblegal-cookie-banner](https://github.com/lauyan-software/weblegal-cookie-banner)**, a free GDPR cookie consent banner, MIT licence: blocks trackers before consent, supports Google Consent Mode v2 and the GPC signal, WCAG 2.2 AA. [Try it](https://weblegal.ai/en/cookie-banner/).
